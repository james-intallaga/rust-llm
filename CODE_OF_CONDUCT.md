# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Contributors should communicate constructively, assume good intent, accept
feedback gracefully, and focus disagreement on ideas and evidence.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public. Report unacceptable behavior privately to `hi@intallaga.com`. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when conduct is harmful to the community.
