# Privacy Policy

**AMMA AI - Forge SDK**

Last updated: January 2026

## Overview

This privacy notice describes how AMMA AI Intallaga Tech ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use applications built with the Forge SDK.

## Key Points

- **On-Device Processing**: The Forge SDK runs AI models entirely on your device. No data is sent to external servers for AI processing.
- **No Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or conversation data.
- **Local Storage Only**: All model files, conversation history, and settings remain on your device.

## What Information We Process

### Model Files
AI model files (GGUF format) are stored locally on your device. These are downloaded from sources you choose (e.g., Hugging Face) and are not transmitted elsewhere.

### Conversation Data
Any text or images you provide to the AI are processed entirely on-device. We do not have access to your conversations.

### Device Information
For crash reporting and diagnostics (if enabled by the app developer), basic device information may be collected:
- Device model and OS version
- App version
- Crash logs

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Delete any locally stored data at any time
- Control what models are downloaded and stored
- Disable any analytics or crash reporting
